Holiday FAQs & Support

Everything you need to know about Christmas shipping, gift wrapping, and holiday returns.

What is the Christmas delivery cut-off?
Standard shipping orders must be placed by December 18. Express Santa Sleigh shipping is available through December 23 for guaranteed Christmas Eve arrival.
Is holiday gift wrapping complimentary?
Yes! Every order includes complimentary red-and-gold gift box packaging with a handcrafted festive satin ribbon and a personalized holiday card.
Can I schedule delivery on a specific date?
Absolutely. During checkout, you can select your desired delivery window, including early surprise deliveries or directly on Christmas Eve.
What is your extended holiday return policy?
All holiday purchases made between November 1 and December 25 can be exchanged or returned with zero restocking fees until January 31.
